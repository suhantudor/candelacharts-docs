# Alerts

**Trend Concepts** provides alert options for key signals. All alerts fire once per bar close.

### Bias Magnet Alerts

#### BM Bull

* **Triggers when:** Bias Magnet trend flips to bullish (confirmed on bar close).
* **Alert message:** `Trend Concepts — Bullish signal (Bias Magnet trend flip)`

#### BM Bear

* **Triggers when:** Bias Magnet trend flips to bearish (confirmed on bar close).
* **Alert message:** `Trend Concepts — Bearish signal (Bias Magnet trend flip)`
