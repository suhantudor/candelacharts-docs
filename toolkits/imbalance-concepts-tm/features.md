# Features

Imbalance Concepts focus on detecting structural inefficiencies and gaps in price delivery, which often act as magnets for future price action or signal major market shifts.

**Available Imbalance Types:**

* Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
* Inversion Fair Value Gaps (IFVG)
* Balanced Price Range (BPR)
* Volume Imbalances (VI)
* Opening Gaps (OG)
* Intraday Gaps (IG)
* New Week Opening Gaps (NWOG)
* New Day Opening Gaps (NDOG)
* Regular Trading Hours Gaps (RTH)
