# Labels

The visual presentation of the Breaker Model is highly customizable to fit any chart aesthetic.

### Labels

* **Labels Size**: Scale the text across the indicator (`Tiny`, `Small`, `Normal`, `Large`).
* **Label Text Color**: Change the color of the text used to identify the setups (e.g., "+Unicorn", "-Breaker") and the R:R projection tags.

### Colors

You can distinctly separate the states of your setups:

* **Active Colors**: Custom background and border colors for valid, live setups that have not yet hit target or invalidation (e.g., bright green for Bullish, bright red for Bearish).
* **Inactive Colors**: The color a setup fades to once it is completed.
* **FVG Tint**: When in Unicorn Mode, the overlapping Fair Value Gap is tinted with a distinct color to highlight the premium entry zone.

### Linework

Customize the `Sweep Line` (tracking the liquidity raid) and the `Projection Line` (R:R targets). Both can be set to solid, dashed, or dotted, with adjustable width and colors.
