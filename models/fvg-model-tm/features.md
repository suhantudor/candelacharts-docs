# Features

The FVG Model is a complete multi-timeframe trading system that automates every step of the FVG-based workflow, from liquidity detection to trade management.

* **Automated FVG Model Detection**: Identifies both Bullish and Bearish FVG models based on HTF liquidity sweeps followed by LTF market structure shifts, eliminating manual multi-timeframe analysis.
* **Multi-Timeframe Pairing**: Automatically pairs your current chart timeframe with a correlated Higher Timeframe (e.g., 5m → 1H), or allows for fully custom LTF/HTF definitions across 25+ preset combinations.
* **HTF-to-LTF Candle Mapping**: Projects Higher Timeframe candles directly onto your Lower Timeframe chart with customizable offset, spacing, width, and coloring — no need to switch timeframes.
* **Fair Value Gap Visualization**: Maps HTF Fair Value Gaps onto the LTF chart, including optional Consequent Encroachment (CE) line and customizable borders for precise entry identification.
* **Market Structure Tracking**: Monitors for Change in State of Delivery (CISD) or Market Structure Shift (MSS) following a liquidity sweep to confirm the model with structural evidence.
* **Standard Deviation Projections**: Automatically plots take-profit and stop-loss targets based on standard deviation calculations anchored from the sweep candle's wick or body.
* **Directional Bias Filtering**: Choose between Automatic (aligns with HTF sweep direction), Neutral, Bullish, or Bearish bias modes to match your trading thesis.
* **Status Lifecycle Management**: Track every model through its lifecycle — Active, Completed, or Failed — with the option to hide failed models for a cleaner chart.
* **Session-Based Time Filtering**: Restrict model detection to specific institutional trading windows: Asia, London, NY AM, NY Launch, NY PM, or a fully Custom session.
* **Secondary HTF Layer**: Optionally enable a second set of HTF candles from an independent timeframe for additional macro context on the same chart.
* **LTF Open/Close Lines**: Display structural Open/Close reference lines on the lower timeframe for enhanced visibility of key levels within each HTF candle.
