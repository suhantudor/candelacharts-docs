# Features

The Composite Risk Index is an institutional-grade macro compass that synthesizes three distinct pillars of market data into one clear, unified Risk Score.

* **Tri-Factor Analysis:** Seamlessly evaluates Volatility, Trend, and Momentum simultaneously. You no longer need to clutter your chart with multiple oscillators to understand the macro environment.
* **Dynamic Regime Compass:** Categorizes the market into four states using a sleek dashboard: Risk-On (Teal), Neutral (Gray), Risk-Off (Orange), and Crisis (Red).
* **External Volatility Integration:** Automatically queries CBOE:VIX for equities and DERIBIT:DVOL for crypto to integrate implied volatility data directly into the index.
* **Buy Zone Highlighting:** Automatically flags your chart's background when the index reaches a deep negative score (the Crisis zone), indicating high-probability accumulation zones.
* **Customizable Alerts:** Features dynamic alert functions that notify you the exact moment the overall market regime shifts, ensuring you never miss a structural change.
