# Confluences

The VIX Risk Z-Score is a powerful macro timing tool, but its signals are drastically improved when paired with price-action confirmation and other structural indicators.

* **Mean Reversion Index**: The ultimate contrarian setup occurs when the VIX Risk Z-Score spikes into "Panic" (Z > 2.0) at the exact same time the Mean Reversion Index plunges into its -2σ Capitulation Buy Zone. This dual-signal confirms both extreme price extension and extreme market fear.
* **Divergences**: Watch for price making a lower low while the VIX Risk Z-Score makes a lower high. This suggests the market is selling off, but the "fear" behind the sell-off is already subsiding, often signaling exhaustion.
* **Macro Credit Model**: If the VIX is spiking but the Macro Credit Model remains in a "Low Risk" regime, the sell-off is likely a normal equity correction, making it a safe dip to buy. If Credit Spreads blow out alongside the VIX, it signifies a deeper structural crisis.
