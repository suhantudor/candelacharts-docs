# Features

The VIX Risk Z-Score transforms complex volatility data into a clear, actionable visual framework right on your TradingView chart.

* **Statistical Z-Score Normalization**: Automatically converts the raw VIX data from FRED into a rolling Z-Score, adjusting to the inherent volatility environment over a customizable deep lookback period (default 365 days).
* **Automated Risk Thresholds**: Features customizable Overbought (Panic) and Oversold (Complacency) thresholds, defaulting to +2.0 and -2.0 standard deviations.
* **Main Chart Candle Overlay**: Seamlessly colors your main chart's price candles based on the VIX Z-Score condition, allowing you to track volatility extremes without taking your eyes off the price action.
* **Dynamic Gradient Fills**: Highlights extreme volatility zones in the oscillator pane using beautiful gradient fills to quickly draw your attention to statistical outliers.
* **Customizable Theming**: Full control over the colors representing Panic (default Blue), Complacency (default Orange), and Neutral (default Gray) market regimes.
