# Features

The Trend Heatmap Matrix is heavily customizable, allowing you to tailor its aggregated signals to your specific trading style and preferred technical components.

* **10-Component Aggregation Engine**: Synthesizes data from Directional Strength, MACD, RSI, Stochastic, CCI, Velocity, Supertrend, VWAP, MFI, and TSI.
* **Trading Profiles**: Quickly configure the internal timeframes of all components by selecting a preset profile (Scalping, Day Trade, Swing, Investment).
* **Toggleable Components**: Individually enable or disable any of the 10 core components. If you don't care about CCI or VWAP, turn them off and the matrix will recalculate its bias without them.
* **Dynamic Bias Dashboard**: An on-chart UI that displays the current aggregated trend state (e.g., "STRONG LONG", "LEAN SHORT", "NEUTRAL") and the bullish/bearish percentage breakdown.
* **Bar Coloring**: Optionally color your main chart's price candles based on the aggregated trend state, giving you instant visual feedback on momentum.
* **Comprehensive Theming**: Includes 12 beautiful built-in color themes (plus a custom option) to seamlessly integrate into your personal chart aesthetic.
