# Features

The Altseason Index comes packed with features and customizable settings to tailor the indicator to your trading style.

* **Composite Modeling**: Aggregates four independent metrics into a single, noise-reduced 0-100 oscillator.
* **Deep Opportunity Highlights**: Automatically flashes vertical chart backgrounds when the index dips below the critical 20 level, pinpointing prime accumulation zones on both the oscillator and main chart.
* **Tiered Zones**: Distinct visual gradients highlight the standard Opportunity zone (under 30) versus the Deep Opportunity zone (under 20).
* **Fully Customizable**: Control component weights, timeframes, symbol inputs, and all visual colors directly from the settings panel.
