# Altseason Index 🔜

The **Altseason Index** is a premium indicator designed for traders and investors looking to navigate the cryptocurrency markets by understanding the flow of capital between Bitcoin and Altcoins.&#x20;

By utilizing a composite model that includes momentum, mean-reversion, and cross-asset comparisons, it provides a clear 0-100 gauge of the altcoin market's current state.

Whether you are looking for deep value accumulation zones or trying to identify extreme risk market tops, the Altseason Index simplifies complex market data into an intuitive and customizable visual oscillator.
