# Confluences

While the Altseason Index is a powerful tool on its own, it is best utilized in confluence with other technical indicators and price action concepts to filter signals and execute high-probability trades.

### 1. Market Structure & Support/Resistance

When the Altseason Index drops below 20 (Deep Opportunity), look for the total altcoin market cap (e.g., `TOTAL3` or `OTHERS`) to test major horizontal support levels or higher timeframe order blocks. A bounce from structural support while the index is heavily oversold provides a highly favorable risk-to-reward ratio for long positions.

### 2. Fair Value Gaps (FVG)

Combine the Altseason Index with FVG models. If the index signals an extreme undervaluation (under 30) and price drops into a bullish FVG (discount array) on a high timeframe, it often signals smart money accumulation before the next leg up in the altcoin market.

### 3. Trend Indicators & Moving Averages

Use trend-following indicators like the 200-day moving average. If the Altseason Index flashes a "Deep Opportunity" signal while the price of your target altcoin remains structurally above its 200 SMA, it suggests a healthy pullback in an overarching bull market—a prime buy-the-dip scenario.

### 4. Bitcoin Dominance (BTC.D)

Monitor Bitcoin Dominance alongside the Altseason Index. An Extreme Risk signal (index > 80) combined with BTC.D bouncing off long-term support heavily confirms an impending capital rotation out of altcoins and back into Bitcoin.

By waiting for multiple technical factors to align with the Altseason Index, you can avoid premature entries during prolonged downtrends and maximize profits during peak altcoin mania.
