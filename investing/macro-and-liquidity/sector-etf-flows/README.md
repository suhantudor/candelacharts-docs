# Sector ETF Flows

The **CandelaCharts - Sector ETF Flows** indicator is a powerful macro-analysis tool designed to track institutional capital rotation across the major segments of the U.S. stock market.

By monitoring the daily inflows and outflows of major SPDR Sector ETFs (such as Technology, Financials, Healthcare, and more), this indicator provides a clear picture of shifting risk appetite and sector leadership. Instead of relying solely on price action, tracking actual capital movement allows investors and swing traders to identify which sectors are accumulating institutional backing and which are facing distribution.
