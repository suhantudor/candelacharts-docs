# Confluences

Sector flow data is highly effective when paired with traditional price action and market breadth indicators.

* **Market Breadth**: Combine sector flows with advance/decline lines or the percentage of stocks above their 50-day moving average. Strong breadth combined with aggressive inflows into multiple sectors confirms a healthy bull market.
* **Relative Strength (RS)**: Use relative strength comparisons (e.g., XLK vs SPY). A sector exhibiting strong price RS that is also backed by massive ETF inflows is a prime candidate for long positions.
* **Index Breakouts**: When major indices like the S\&P 500 or NASDAQ break out to new highs, check the sector flows. A breakout driven by broad, multi-sector inflows is structurally sound, whereas a breakout driven by only one sector (with others seeing outflows) may be fragile.
