# Features

The Sector ETF Flows indicator provides a comprehensive suite of analytical tools to dissect institutional money movement across the stock market, offering highly customizable visualizations and deep data metrics.

* **Comprehensive Sector Tracking**: Monitors major SPDR Sector ETFs including Technology (XLK), Financials (XLF), Healthcare (XLV), Consumer Discretionary (XLY), and more, plus XME for metals exposure.
* **Dual Plot Modes**: Switch between "Inflows & Outflows" for absolute capital movement, or "Dominance" to see the relative market share of specific sectors.
* **Flexible Data Formatting**: View dashboard metrics as raw Money (USD), Z-Scores (for statistical extremes), Percentages, or Ratios.
* **Multi-Timeframe Dashboard**: A built-in panel that displays flow data across 7D, 14D, 30D, 90D, 180D, and 365D lookback horizons.
* **Customizable Smoothing**: Fine-tune the SMA Mean Length for background plots and the Z-Score Length to adjust sensitivity based on your trading style.
