# Features

The Bitcoin ETF Flows indicator provides a robust suite of analytical tools to dissect institutional money movement, offering highly customizable visualizations and comprehensive data metrics.

* **Multi-ETF Tracking**: Monitors 10 major Spot Bitcoin ETFs including BlackRock (IBIT), Fidelity (FBTC), Grayscale (GBTC), ARK 21Shares (ARKB), and more.
* **Dual Plot Modes**: Switch between "Inflows & Outflows" to see absolute capital movement, or "Dominance" to see the relative market share of specific ETFs.
* **Flexible Data Formatting**: View data in raw Money (USD), Coins (BTC), Z-Scores (for statistical significance), or Percentages.
* **Multi-Timeframe Dashboard**: A comprehensive built-in dashboard that displays flow data across 7D, 14D, 30D, 90D, 180D, and 365D lookback periods.
* **Customizable Smoothing & Baselines**: Adjust the SMA Mean Length for background plots and the Z-Score Length to tailor the indicator's sensitivity to your specific timeframe.
