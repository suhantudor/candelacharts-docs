# Bitcoin ETF Flows

The **CandelaCharts - Bitcoin ETF Flows** indicator is a comprehensive tracking tool designed to monitor institutional capital movement across major Spot Bitcoin ETFs.

Since the launch of Spot Bitcoin ETFs, institutional flows have become a primary driver of Bitcoin's price action. By tracking the inflows and outflows of major funds like BlackRock (IBIT), Fidelity (FBTC), and Grayscale (GBTC), traders can gauge true institutional demand, measure market sentiment, and identify leading indicators for broader market trends.
