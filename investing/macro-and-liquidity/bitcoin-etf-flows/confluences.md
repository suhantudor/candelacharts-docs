# Confluences

Institutional flow data is most powerful when combined with price-based and on-chain indicators.

* **Breakouts and Breakdowns**: A price breakout accompanied by a massive surge in ETF inflows strongly validates the move. Conversely, a breakout with zero or negative ETF flows may indicate a retail-driven "fakeout."
* **Support and Resistance**: If Bitcoin price approaches a major support level and ETF inflows begin to spike, it suggests institutions are defending the level (buying the dip).
* **On-Chain Metrics**: Pairing ETF Flows with broader on-chain metrics like Exchange Balances or Miner Outflows provides a complete picture of supply and demand dynamics across the entire Bitcoin ecosystem.
