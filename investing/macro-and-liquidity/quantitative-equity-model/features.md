# Features

The Quantitative Equity Model offers a deeply customizable engine that analyzes five critical dimensions of the market, allowing you to tailor your risk exposure precisely to your investment philosophy.

* **The Five-Pillar Framework**: Analyzes Market Regime (35%), Risk Metrics (25%), Valuation (20%), Sentiment (15%), and Macro (5%) to determine optimal allocation.
* **Automated Crisis Detection**: Employs hard-coded thresholds for catastrophic conditions (extreme VIX, massive drawdowns, or credit spread blowouts) to instantly de-risk the portfolio.
* **Portfolio Risk Scaling**: Allows you to define maximum acceptable portfolio drawdowns and target volatility, mathematically constraining your exposure.
* **Visual Buy Zones**: Clearly marks persistent "Buy Zones" with lower-bound chart fills (0-40% equity) and background highlights, representing periods of maximum fear and generational buying opportunities.
* **Comprehensive Dashboard**: An on-chart UI that displays real-time status for all five pillars, risk targets, and crisis monitors.
* **Highly Customizable Engine**: Full control over pillar weighting, crisis thresholds, baseline aggressiveness (Risk-Averse, Balanced, Risk-Seeking, Adaptive), and underlying calculation symbols.
