# Quantitative Equity Model

The **CandelaCharts - Quantitative Equity Model** is an advanced, institutional-grade asset allocation indicator. Rather than relying on a single technical or fundamental aspect, this model synthesizes diverse market data streams to dynamically calculate an optimal portfolio equity allocation (from 0% to 100%).

The primary goal of the model is twofold: to maximize equity exposure and compounding returns during sustained bullish trends, and to aggressively de-risk and preserve capital when the market enters high-risk, volatile, or crisis environments.
