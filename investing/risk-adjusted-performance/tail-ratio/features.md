# Features

The Tail Ratio indicator comes equipped with a suite of highly customizable tools to help you accurately measure, smooth, and interpret the asymmetry of market returns in real-time.

* **Dynamic Percentile Calculation**: Utilizes customizable upper and lower percentiles (e.g., 90th and 10th) to isolate the true extremes of the return distribution.
* **Log or Simple Returns**: Toggle between simple returns or logarithmic returns to match your specific quantitative analysis preferences.
* **Smoothing Engine**: Built-in exponential moving average (EMA) smoothing to filter out erratic micro-volatility and provide a clean, readable oscillator.
* **Visual Thresholds**: Clear visual demarcations (Mid, Upper Guide, Lower Guide) to quickly identify when the distribution becomes highly asymmetric.
* **On-Chart Interpretation Table**: A built-in UI panel that displays the exact Tail Ratio value and its current statistical interpretation (Positive Skew, Normal, or Negative Skew).
