# Confluences

The Tail Ratio is a powerful context tool that pairs exceptionally well with traditional directional indicators.

* **Trend Following**: Use the Tail Ratio to validate trend strength. A strong uptrend accompanied by a Right-tailed (Positive Skew) reading suggests the trend is supported by robust upside momentum and is structurally sound.
* **Mean Reversion / Reversals**: Look for divergences. If price is making new highs but the Tail Ratio shifts to a Left-tailed (Negative Skew) regime, it warns that underlying downside risk is building, potentially signaling an impending reversal.
* **Volatility Breakouts**: When price breaks out of a consolidation range, a corresponding surge in the Tail Ratio can confirm the direction of the breakout has strong structural backing.
