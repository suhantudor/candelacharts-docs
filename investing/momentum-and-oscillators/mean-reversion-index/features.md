# Features

The Mean Reversion Index combines rigorous statistical modeling with highly customizable visual cues to give you an actionable read on market extremes.

* **Dynamic Z-Score Normalization**: Automatically converts the raw price distance from the moving average into a standardized Z-Score, adjusting to the asset's inherent volatility over a deep lookback period (e.g., 730 days).
* **Automated Buy Zones**: Visually highlights the indicator pane (and optionally the main chart background) when the index drops below the -2σ extreme, signaling a statistically rare buying opportunity.
* **Statistical Banding**: Features clear, color-coded background fills separating the +1σ to -1σ (normal distribution) and the 1σ to 2σ (extended) zones for instant visual context.
* **Fractal Map (Time Cycles)**: Includes an optional overlay that tracks and visualizes historical cycle lengths between major market extremes, aiding in temporal market timing.
* **Comprehensive Theming System**: Offers 12 built-in color themes (plus a custom option) to seamlessly match any chart aesthetic you prefer.
